04 disc delivery

type
own project · subscription concept
team
solo
scope
brand · web · packaging · editorial
year
2026, ongoing

the idea

streaming makes discovery feel passive. disc delivery slows it down: every month subscribers get a cd with 12 independent artists, a booklet about them and an art print. our mission? to let music find you again

what i did

i designed a logo where the cd itself becomes the mark, paired fraunces with dm mono and a strong blue, and planned a website for subscribing, meeting artists and submitting music. around it sit the booklet, packaging, a zine called delivered, pricing and a kickstarter campaign.

what i learned

the idea changed many times, from signed cds and info cards to the curated compilation. iterating taught me to design the whole journey, from the website to opening the box, so every part feels like the same brand.

a work in progress

disc delivery is an ongoing project, which is destined to go through countless changes. there's already been multiple variations of the logo, as well as general brand identity. what you see is not the final product (especially the ai-generated images).
