The Cyber Castle

The Cyber Castle isn't just a name—it's a fortress of knowledge and protection in the digital realm. We built it for curious minds who want to learn cybersecurity without fear or anxiety.

In our castle, we blend medieval fantasy with the modern challenges of the internet. Knights and dragons, phishing attacks and scams - they all come together to create an engaging learning experience.

We welcome you to the Cyber Castle to learn and to become a guardian of your own digital kingdom.

The Cyber Knights Course

Our course is rated E for Everyone Each week, you'll receive a lesson that teaches you cybersecurity skills.

Why weekly lessons? Spaced learning is more effective than cramming. One lesson per week keeps you motivated, gives you time to practice, and helps the knowledge stick.

We cover phishing attacks, password security, safe browsing, and much more. Each lesson is wrapped in fantasy to keep it engaging and fun.

By the end of the course, you'll be a true Cyber Knight!

Our Mission

We (the team) are a small group of chronic internet users. We've experienced the dangers of the internet, and we want to help.

After watching too many friends and fellow students fall victim to digital threats, we realized something had to change. We realized cybersecurity education was either too complex or too boring.

Our mission is simple: Transform ordinary students into brave Cyber Knights who can protect themselves in the vast kingdom of the internet.
