the idea

a choose-your-own-adventure game that teaches students about phishing. you play a would-be cyber knight helping the king fight phishing dragons, and your choices decide how it ends.

how it works

seven scenes of images and text, switched dynamically with javascript. open the suspicious letter without inspecting it and you lose; check it first and you move on. a toggle swaps the fantasy font for an easier-to-read one.

what i learned

branching logic and dom manipulation in plain javascript, and that wrapping a dry subject in a story makes it far easier to remember.
