the brief

small, minority-run and subculture businesses often lack the money and access to build a credible brand. unhush is the agency we invented for them, with one promise: less noise, more you.

what we did

we built the concept on a swot analysis, a value proposition and personas, then made a brand guide, a wordpress site with seo-tuned headings and metadata, and fuzzy, a mascot who explains the service in small animated speech bubbles. we tested the prototype with think-aloud tests and the brand guide with a survey.

what i learned

users liked the look and understood the slogan, but fuzzy got in the way of the text and the hero video's sound annoyed people. a fun idea still has to earn its place on the page.
